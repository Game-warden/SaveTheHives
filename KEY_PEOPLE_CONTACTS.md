- **Citizen Science Club at NC State** — active undergrad student org, open to grad students/faculty too.
- **Wolfpack Citizen Science Challenge** — annual event, NCSU Libraries + the Leadership in Public Science cluster, featuring one faculty-led project each spring.

Why it's a strong fit: SaveTheHives' SciStarter listing already targets "College, Graduate students" and pitches ready-made field sites — this program is the actual machinery that turns that pitch into real NC State student participation.
