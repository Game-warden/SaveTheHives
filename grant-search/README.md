## Fiscal sponsor research

`fiscal-sponsor-outreach.md` (added 2026-08-23) — answers to Ronnie's
follow-up questions after reviewing the One Hive Foundation FAQ:

- **NC State is not a viable fiscal sponsor.** Structural reasoning, not a
  guess: fiscal sponsorship extends 501(c)(3) status to an *unaffiliated*
  outside project; NC State's grants machinery only administers funding
  for its own faculty as PI. Ronnie isn't NC State-affiliated, so this
  collapses back into the existing Path A (funding flows to Tarpy's grant,
  not a Ronnie-controlled budget) rather than being a fourth candidate.
- **Fee research on the three named candidates** (Players Philanthropy
  Fund, Social Good Fund, Third Sector New England) — PPF ~6% flat, Social
  Good Fund tiered 6.5–8% at SaveTheHives' likely size plus a small
  monthly fee, TSNE's fee isn't published and its own site states a 2–3
  month application process, which likely rules it out for the Sept 15
  One Hive Foundation deadline on timing alone.
- Includes a ready-to-send outreach email (PPF / Social Good Fund
  version) — not yet sent.
