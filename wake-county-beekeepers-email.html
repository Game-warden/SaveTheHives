I'm reaching out to introduce (or reintroduce) SaveTheHives — a citizen-science project mapping feral/wild honeybee colonies, originally launched back in 2008 with a lot of records concentrated right here in the Triangle.

I've spent the last several months rebuilding it from scratch.
